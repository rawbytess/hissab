Hissab supports unit conversions for most popular units, and then some.

To convert from one unit to another, type your expressions in this
format:

> \{value} \{unit} to \{destination unit}

The 'to' can be replaced with 'in' as well.

> \{value} \{unit} in \{destination unit}

<HissabExample
  text="50 meters to miles"
  classes="hissab-example hissab-example-inline hissab-example-correct"
/>

<HissabExample
  text={`Convert 50 meters to miles\n50 meters to 5 miles`}
  classes="hissab-example hissab-example-inline hissab-example-wrong"
/>

You can perform various mathematical operations with values of different
units seamlessly. Hissab will choose the first value's unit as base and
convert all other values to that base unit to calculate final result.

<HissabExample
  text="50 meters + 15 miles - 10 km"
  classes="hissab-example hissab-example-inline"
/>

However, if you want to force final result to use certain unit, simply
add 'to \{final unit}' at the end of the expression.

<HissabExample
  text="50 meters + 15 miles - 10 km to yards"
  classes="hissab-example hissab-example-inline"
/>

### Breakdown units

The results with large value will be broken down into smaller valued units as seen above.
You can also explicitly mention what units the value should be broken down.

<HissabExample
  text="50 meters + 15 miles - 10 km to miles,yards,feet"
  classes="hissab-example hissab-example-inline"
/>
