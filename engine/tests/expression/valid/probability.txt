# Probability — P() literal, complement/AND/OR/xor via ~ & | xor, and helpers.
# Values < 1 render to 3 significant figures (see NumberToken.formatResult).

# P() defines a probability; accepts a fraction or a percent.
["p(0.1)", "0.1"]
["p(10%)", "0.1"]
["probability(0.4)", "0.4"]

# ~ is complement, & is intersection (AND), | is union (OR), xor is "exactly one".
["~p(0.2)", "0.8"]
["p(0.5) & p(0.3)", "0.15"]
["p(0.5) | p(0.3)", "0.65"]
["p(0.5) xor p(0.3)", "0.5"]

# Tagged results chain; & binds tighter than | (AND before OR).
["p(0.2) & p(0.3) & p(0.1)", "0.006"]
["p(0.1) | p(0.2) & p(0.5)", "0.19"]

# A bare number combined with a probability is treated as a probability.
["p(0.2) & 0.3", "0.06"]

# Plain arithmetic works on the underlying values.
["p(0.3) - p(0.1)", "0.2"]

# Higher-level helpers.
["conditional(0.12, 0.3)", "0.4"]
["bayes(0.01, 0.9, 0.05)", "0.154"]
["odds(0.75)", "3"]
["probability from odds(3)", "0.75"]
["binomial(10, 3, 0.5)", "0.117"]
["expected value(10, 0.5, 20, 0.5)", "15"]
